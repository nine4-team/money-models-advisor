I’d think about this as CAC first, not as a fixed ad budget.

With your current numbers:
- CAC is about `$1,000`
- first-30-day gross profit is about `$10,000`
- payback is about `1 month`

So my read is:
- `~$1,000 CAC` is the current benchmark if ads perform like your referral channel
- `~$10,000 CAC` is the theoretical 1-month payback ceiling, but I would not plan around that
- actual ad spend should be `target customers × target CAC`

Example:
- 5 customers at a `$1,000 CAC` = about `$5,000` in ad spend
- 10 customers = about `$10,000` in ad spend
