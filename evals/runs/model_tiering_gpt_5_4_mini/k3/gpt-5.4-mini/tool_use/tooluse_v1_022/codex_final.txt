I’d frame it like this for this business:

- `Attraction offer`: the `$597 STR Design Diagnostic`. Its job is to get the prospect in the door, lower friction, and qualify the project.
- `Upsell`: the `$5,000 pre-designed room package`. Its job is to increase average order value after someone is already engaged and buying.

So the distinction is: attraction offer acquires and qualifies; upsell monetizes the same customer further after the first purchase.
