Fulfillment cost matters because ads buy revenue, but fulfillment cost determines how much profit is left after delivery. That leftover gross profit is what pays back CAC. If delivery is too expensive, an ad can look good on leads or sales and still fail on cash because the margin is too thin or too slow.

In your snapshot, CAC is `$1,000` and first-30-day gross profit is `$10,000`, so payback is `1.0 month`. That means ads can work here because the business recovers acquisition cost quickly. The real constraint is whether fulfillment stays efficient enough to preserve that gross profit as volume rises.
