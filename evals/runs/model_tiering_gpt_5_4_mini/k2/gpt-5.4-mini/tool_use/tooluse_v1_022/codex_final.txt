The attraction offer is the front-end piece that gets the lead to engage; the upsell is the next offer after that, meant to increase profit per customer [make-your-money-model:1] [upsell-offers:0]. In this case, the `$597` STR Design Diagnostic is the attraction offer, the `$5,000` pre-designed room packages are the upsell, and the `$15,000` full-service design is the core offer. Upsells can be quantity, quality, or complementary add-ons, but the point is always to maximize profit after the first conversion [upsell-offers:1].
