Fulfillment cost is what you subtract from revenue to get gross profit, and gross profit is the only money available to repay what an ad spends to win a customer (CAC). Without fulfillment cost you cannot compute gross profit, and without gross profit you cannot know your payback period, so you cannot tell whether paid ads can work.
