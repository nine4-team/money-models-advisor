Gross profit means the money left after subtracting the direct cost of delivering what you sold.

Example: if a customer pays `$100` and it costs `$35` to make, fulfill, ship, or deliver it, gross profit is `$65`. It is not final profit because overhead like rent, software, admin payroll, and taxes still come after that.
