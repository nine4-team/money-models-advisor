Gross profit is what I have left after subtracting the direct cost of delivering what I sold from the revenue I brought in.

In plain English:
- Revenue = money in
- Direct costs = the stuff it takes to make or deliver the thing
- Gross profit = what’s left before overhead like rent, admin payroll, or marketing

Example: if I bring in $100 and it costs me $40 to deliver, gross profit is $60.
