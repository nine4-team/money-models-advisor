Gross profit means the money left after you subtract the direct cost of delivering what you sold.

Example: if a customer pays `$100` and it costs `$40` to fulfill the product or service, gross profit is `$60`. It is not final take-home profit yet because it excludes overhead like rent, admin, software, taxes, and owner pay.
