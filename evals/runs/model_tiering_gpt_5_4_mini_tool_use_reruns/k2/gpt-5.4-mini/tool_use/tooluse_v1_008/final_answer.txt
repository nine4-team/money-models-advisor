Economically, this is a strong cash-payback model: CAC is about $1,000, first-30-day gross profit is about $10,000, and payback is 1.0 month. In plain terms, the business can recover acquisition cost very quickly, with roughly 10x gross-profit coverage in the first 30 days. The main caveat is that there is no recurring gross profit/continuity layer shown yet, so the economics are being carried by upfront project gross profit rather than lifetime retention.
