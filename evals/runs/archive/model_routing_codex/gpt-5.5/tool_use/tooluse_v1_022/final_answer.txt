In this situation, the attraction offer is the entry point: the $597 STR Design Diagnostic. Its job is to get the right short-term-rental or second-home owner to raise their hand, experience your thinking, and become qualified for the real design conversation. Money Models frames this as step one: the thing that gets leads/customers to engage on the front end [make-your-money-model:1].

The upsell is what you offer after that first commitment or after the core service reveals the next natural need. In your current stack, the $5K pre-designed room packages are not mainly lead generation; they are an ascension/profit lever for someone already moving through the design relationship. The clean test is: "Did this offer get them in the door, or is it the next thing that goes with what they already bought?" Upsells work best when they are more, better, or complementary to the thing they just got [upsell-offers:0] [upsell-offers:1].

So practically: Diagnostic = trust-building front door. Room packages = additional monetization/fulfillment path after intent is established. These can blur in edge cases, because Money Models allows stacking and reusing mechanisms, but I would keep the roles separate here so the diagnostic stays easy to say yes to and the room package is positioned as the obvious next step for owners who want implementation help [make-your-money-model:0].
