Client-financed acquisition means the client’s first project cash flow should pay back the cost of getting them in the door fast enough that growth can keep funding itself. In your numbers, CAC is about k and first-30-day gross profit is about k, so payback is roughly 1 month. I’d treat that as self-liquidating acquisition: the main constraint is cash timing and fulfillment capacity, not whether the economics work. If the acquisition spend has to be fronted before the client cash arrives, you still need working capital, but the unit economics are strong enough that each client can finance the next one.
