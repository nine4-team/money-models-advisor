Fulfillment cost matters because ads are paid back from gross profit, not revenue. If it takes too much to deliver the job, a sale can look strong on paper but leave too little cash to recover CAC.

On your current numbers, CAC is $1,000 and first-30-day gross profit is $10,000, so payback is about 1 month. That means ads can work as long as fulfillment still leaves enough gross profit after delivery.
