Think of your ad ceiling in terms of payback, not a flat budget. The three levers are CAC (what you pay to get a client), gross profit (~$10K in the first 30 days at 76.9% margin), and payback period. Right now CAC is ~$1K from referral partners, so each client returns ~$10 for every $1 spent and pays back in about 1 month. That means you can comfortably test paid ads up to a CAC well above $1K and still recover the cash fast. A practical rule: as long as a new client is gross-profit positive inside ~30 days (CAC recovered within the first-sale window), you can keep spending. So set a target CAC ceiling per the first-30-day gross profit you are willing to reinvest, start small to find your real paid CAC, and scale spend as long as payback stays inside that window.
