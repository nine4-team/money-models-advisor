Economically you are in a strong, client-financed position. Each new client costs about $1,000 to acquire and returns roughly $10,000 in gross profit within the first 30 days, so your payback period is about 1 month and you recover acquisition cost almost immediately. With a 77% gross margin and 10x first-month GP-to-CAC, acquisition is effectively self-funding [§1.1.b][§1.2.c]: every client pays back their own acquisition cost in the first month and throws off cash you can reinvest into more acquisition. The practical read: you have substantial headroom to spend more to acquire clients and can afford a paid front-end test, since you would still hit payback well inside 30 days even at a meaningfully higher CAC.
