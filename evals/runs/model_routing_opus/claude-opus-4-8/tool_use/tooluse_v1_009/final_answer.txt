Fulfillment cost is what it costs me to deliver the work, and gross profit is your revenue minus that cost. Ads are paid for out of gross profit, not revenue. So whether an ad can work is really: does the gross profit from a new client cover what it cost to acquire them (CAC), and how fast (payback period)? If I only looked at your 15k price and ignored delivery cost, I would overstate how much you can spend to win a client. With your numbers, fulfillment leaves about 10k of 30-day gross profit against a 1k CAC, so payback is roughly 1 month and you have a lot of room to spend on acquisition. Without the fulfillment cost I can not size that room, which is why I need it before saying ads work [how-businesses-make-money:2][payback-period:0][gross-profit:4].
