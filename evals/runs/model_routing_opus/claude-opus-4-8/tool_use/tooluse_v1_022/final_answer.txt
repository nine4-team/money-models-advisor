In your money model these sit at different points in the customer journey. The attraction offer (your  STR Design Diagnostic) is the front-end: a low-priced offer whose job is to acquire a new customer and get them in the door [offer-types:4][upsell-offers:0]. The upsell (your K pre-designed room packages) comes after someone has already bought, raising the value of an existing customer rather than winning a new one [offer-types:4]. So the diagnostic is how a stranger becomes a client; the room packages are how an existing client spends more. They are sequenced attraction first, then core offer, then upsell [make-your-money-model:1].
