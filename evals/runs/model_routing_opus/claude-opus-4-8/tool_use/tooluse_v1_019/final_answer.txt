Here client-financed acquisition means your front-end revenue covers what it costs to win a client, so growth funds itself instead of draining cash. Your numbers already qualify: CAC is ~$1,000 and you make ~$10,000 gross profit in the first 30 days, so 30-day GP is roughly 10x CAC and payback is about 1 month [context:1][how-businesses-make-money:2]. The CFA test is making more than 2x CAC in gross profit within ~30 days; you clear that easily [cfa:2][cfa:0]. Practically: each closed client returns far more cash in month one than it cost to acquire, so you can reinvest that surplus into more acquisition (e.g. spending into the $597 Design Diagnostic to feed the $15K core offer) without waiting on later payments. The constraint on how fast you scale is volume and delivery capacity, not acquisition cash.
