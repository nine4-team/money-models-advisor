Offer a rollover upsell from the $597 STR Design Diagnostic into the $5,000 pre-designed room package.

The clean version is: “If you book your Priority Room Package within 7 days of the diagnostic review, we credit your full $597 diagnostic fee toward implementation.” That makes the diagnostic feel like paid discovery, not a dead-end purchase, and it turns the next step into the obvious fix for whatever the audit reveals.

I would not lead with a generic discount or a broad full-service pitch first. Use the diagnostic to identify the highest-ROI room or listing weakness, then prescribe the matching room package. The Money Models principle is that after an attraction offer creates customers and cash, the upsell should maximize 30-day profit and solve the next problem revealed by the first offer [upsell-offers:1]. The build sequence is attraction offer, then upsell, then downsell and continuity as needed [make-your-money-model:2]. A rollover structure is especially natural here because the prior purchase can be credited toward the next larger offer, as long as the larger offer still has profit after the credit [rollover-upsell:6].
