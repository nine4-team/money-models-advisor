Yes. I would treat this as a Classic Upsell: “you can’t have X without Y.” They bought coaching, and the coaching reveals that training alone is not enough for the result they want. Nutrition support becomes the next natural thing that helps the core offer work better, not a random add-on [classic-upsell:5].

The clean version is: after they enroll, diagnose whether nutrition is the constraint, then prescribe the matching add-on. For example: “Based on your goals and schedule, training will get you part of the way there, but your food structure is the limiter. I’d add nutrition coaching so the 12 weeks produce the result we’re aiming for.”

I would not position it as “do you want to buy more?” I’d position it as “this is the support that goes with the outcome.” The source pattern says upsells work best when they solve the next problem the first offer reveals, and fail when they are offered too early, feel unrelated, or try to solve the same problem again [upsell-offers:1] [upsell-offers:2].

For your business, the likely next-offer pattern is:

1. Core offer: 12-week coached transformation.
2. Trigger: client enrolls and assessment shows food is the bottleneck.
3. Upsell: nutrition support, meal planning, macro/accountability layer, or done-with-you food review.
4. Framing: “This is what makes the coaching work faster and more predictably.”

I’d make it modular but prescribed: not everyone needs the same nutrition depth, but when they do need it, it should feel like the obvious complement to training. That is the natural “fries and Coke” for this offer stack.
