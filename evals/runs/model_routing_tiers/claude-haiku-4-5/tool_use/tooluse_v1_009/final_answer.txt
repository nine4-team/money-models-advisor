Fulfillment cost (the cost to deliver your service or product) directly determines whether paid acquisition is viable. Here's why: Gross profit is what remains after you subtract your cost to deliver from what you charge. That gross profit must exceed your CAC within 30 days for you to afford ads without spending your own cash. In your case, you charge $15,000 for a full-service design, and with $10,000 in 30-day gross profit against a $1,000 CAC, you're at a 1-month payback. That works because your fulfillment cost is low enough relative to your price. If fulfillment cost were higher—say you had to pay Lisa more or hire additional installers—your gross profit would shrink. Lower gross profit means a longer payback period, which eventually makes paid ads unaffordable.
