These rules govern the active query-generation and retrieval path. The older
`source_need_rules.md` remains frozen for the source-need planning eval and historical

## When to search

- Search when the answer needs Money Models support for a concept, comparison,

  different kinds of evidence, run two searches instead of mixing them into one query.
